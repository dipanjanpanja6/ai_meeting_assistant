# Electric Vehicles

Battery electric vehicles (EVs) use an electric motor powered by a battery pack instead of an internal combustion engine. Electric drivetrains convert around 80 to 90 percent of battery energy into motion, while petrol engines convert only about 20 to 30 percent of fuel energy.

Over their full life cycle, EVs generally produce lower greenhouse gas emissions than petrol cars, even though making the battery causes extra emissions at the start. The advantage grows as the electricity grid gets cleaner.

Barriers include purchase price, charging access for people without driveways, and range anxiety. Managed or "smart" charging, which shifts charging to times of low demand or high renewable output, can turn the growing EV fleet into a flexible load that helps the grid instead of straining it.
