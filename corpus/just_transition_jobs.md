# Jobs and the Just Transition

The clean energy sector has become a major employer. Solar installation, wind turbine manufacturing and servicing, battery production, building retrofits and grid construction all create jobs, and clean energy employment worldwide now exceeds employment in fossil fuels.

The new jobs are not always in the same places, or for the same people, as the jobs that are lost. Coal mining communities in particular can suffer when mines and plants close.

A "just transition" refers to policies that support affected workers and regions: retraining programmes, pension protection, economic diversification funds, and locating new clean energy projects in former fossil fuel areas. Such policies are also important for maintaining public support for climate action.
