# Case Study: Battery Storage in California

California has deployed grid batteries faster than almost anywhere else. Its installed battery capacity grew from a few hundred megawatts in 2019 to more than ten gigawatts by 2024.

The batteries charge from abundant midday solar and discharge in the early evening, when solar output falls and demand peaks. This has reduced the state's reliance on gas peaker plants during evening hours and helped manage heat waves that previously threatened rolling blackouts.

The California experience shows how short-duration lithium-ion storage directly addresses the solar "duck curve". It also shows the limits: four-hour batteries do not solve multi-day shortfalls, and the state still relies on imports, hydro and gas for those periods.
