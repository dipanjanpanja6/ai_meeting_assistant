# Hydropower

Hydropower generates electricity from flowing or falling water and is the largest source of renewable electricity worldwide. Dams with reservoirs can store water and adjust output quickly, which makes them a flexible partner for wind and solar.

Hydropower has environmental and social costs. Large dams can flood ecosystems and communities, block fish migration, and emit methane from decomposing vegetation in reservoirs, especially in the tropics.

Droughts linked to climate change have reduced hydropower output in several regions, showing that relying heavily on a single weather-dependent source carries risks. Most of the best large dam sites in developed countries are already in use.
