# Energy Efficiency

Energy efficiency means getting the same service, such as a warm home or a lit room, while using less energy. It is often called the "first fuel" because the cheapest energy is the energy that is never needed.

Examples include building insulation, LED lighting, efficient motors in industry, and better appliances. LED bulbs use about 75 to 80 percent less electricity than incandescent bulbs for the same light.

Efficiency also reduces the size of the clean energy system that must be built: fewer panels, turbines and grid upgrades are needed when demand is lower. The "rebound effect", where cheaper energy services lead people to use more of them, can offset part of the savings.
